How to Play

1. Turn-Based 1v1: Player 1 (X) and Player 2 (O) take turns claiming boxes on a 3x3 grid.

2. Criteria: Each box requires a basketball player who matches both the Row and Column criteria. Country criteria refer to the player's country of birth.

3. Shot Clock: When the shot clock runs down to 0, you commit a turnover and forfeit your turn to the opponent.

4. Turnovers: An invalid answer or picking a player who was already used forfeits your turn immediately.

5. Win: First player to get 3 in a row horizontally, vertically, or diagonally wins.
