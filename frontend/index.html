How To Play Basketball XOX

1
Tic-Tac-Toe Rules: 2 players (X and O) compete to get 3 claimed boxes in a horizontal, vertical, or diagonal row.
2
Basketball Criteria: To claim a box, you must name a player who satisfies both the Row criterion and the Column criterion (e.g. played for Team A + Team B, or Team A + from Country C).
3
Turn Turnover: If you guess a player that doesn't match both criteria or was already picked, you commit a foul and forfeit your turn to the opponent!
4
No Re-use: Each basketball player can only be picked once per match!
