Rules & Instructions

1
Objective: Be the first player to form a horizontal, vertical, or diagonal line of 3 claimed boxes.
2
Criteria Matching: Click an open box and name a player who satisfies both the Row and Column requirements (e.g. played for both franchises, or played for Team X and represents Country Y).
3
Turnovers: If your guess is incorrect or you name a player already drafted this game, you commit a foul and your turn is forfeited to your opponent.
4
No Duplicates: Each basketball player can only be used once per match.
