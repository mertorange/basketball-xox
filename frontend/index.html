        The answers

        Every player who fit each box, the best known first.

        How to play

        1. Two players, on one device or in an online room. Team A plays X, Team B plays O; the first move alternates between grids.
        2. Pick an open box and name a player who fits both its row and its column.
        3. Country criteria mean the player's country of birth. Awards, draft slots, colleges, eras and career totals work the same way: the player must fit both.
        4. A wrong name, a player already used, or an expired shot clock is a turnover: the turn passes.
        5. Three boxes in a row, column or diagonal wins. Nine boxes without a line is a draw.
        6. A match is a series: best of 1, 3, 5 or 7 grids. The first to win more than half takes it; drawn grids don't count.
        7. Stuck? Skip turn passes the ball to your opponent. Draw this grid writes the grid off and deals a new one: online the other player has to say yes; on one device, press it twice.
