      How to play

      1. Two players share the device. Team A plays X, Team B plays O.
      2. Pick an open box and name a player who fits both its row and its column.
      3. Country criteria mean the player's country of birth. Awards, draft slots, colleges, eras and career totals work the same way: the player must fit both.
      4. A wrong name, a player already used, or an expired shot clock is a turnover: the turn passes.
      5. Three boxes in a row, column or diagonal wins. Nine boxes without a line is a draw.
